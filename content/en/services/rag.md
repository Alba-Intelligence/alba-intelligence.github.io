# Retrieval-Augmented Generation

We design RAG systems that retrieve relevant documents and generate answers from your data.

## Document Retrieval

We build systems that search and retrieve relevant documents from your knowledge base. These systems index your data for fast access.

## Answer Generation

We create models that generate answers based on retrieved documents. These answers are factual and based on your data.

## Real-time Updates

We design systems that update automatically as your data changes. This ensures answers are always current.
